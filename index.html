      Meet-the-team filming brief

      Short phone videos that introduce the Warragul team. We turn them into reels and stories for the new Instagram and Facebook pages. For launch we use the team videos we already have from Officer, so there's no deadline. Film these once everyone has settled in at Warragul.

      What to film

      VideoWhoLengthWhat to cover
      1. Welcome to WarragulShannon30–45 secWho you are, that Your Body Hub is opening in Warragul inside Universal Athlete Centre, and that the next videos introduce the team.
      2. Meet the teamTash, James, Alana, Shannon and Christine, one video each20–30 sec eachYour name and role. Two or three things people usually come to see you about. Something you enjoy about your work, or a local connection. End with "Come and see me at Your Body Hub Warragul."
      3. B-rollEveryone5–10 sec clipsShort silent clips of each person at work: setting up a room, demonstrating an exercise on a team member, walking into Universal Athlete Centre, the gym space. Two or three clips each.

      Talk naturally. These are prompts, not a script, and a couple of takes is normal. Christine can film hers when she's back from leave.

      How to film

      • Hold the phone vertically, at eye level, on a stand or propped up if you can.
      • Face a window for natural light. Avoid standing with a bright window behind you.
      • Pick a quiet room, and a tidy background. Your Body Hub signage or a treatment room behind you is ideal.
      • Wear your Your Body Hub uniform.
      • Leave a second of silence before and after you speak, so we can cut cleanly.
      • No filters and no music. We add captions, music and the Warragul branding.

      Please avoid

      Health advertising rules (AHPRA) apply to every post, so:

      • No promises of results, such as "fix", "cure" or "pain-free". "Help with" and "support" are fine.
      • No patients on camera unless they've given written consent. A team member makes a great stand-in.
      • No patient testimonials or stories about someone's treatment.
      • No prices or offers unless we've agreed them with Tara first.

      Sending them to us

      Upload the videos to the shared Warragul folder in Google Drive (videos in Raw Videos, photos in Raw Images). Large video files upload more easily there than on this page. Put the person's name in the file name, for example "Tash - meet the team.mp4". Let us know on the What we need from you tab once they're in, and we'll take it from there. Nothing goes live until you've approved it.
